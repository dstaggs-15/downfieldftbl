DOWNFIELD 2

Want the easy setup? Open Install Downfield.exe and click INSTALL GAME.
You'll get Downfield 2.exe on your desktop. That's what you open to play.
The installer already contains the game. You don't need to download anything else or have an admin account.

If you'd rather skip setup, open the Downfield folder and run Downfield.exe.
This is a Windows 10/11 game for a 64-bit PC. It uses Windows' .NET Framework.
This isn't a Mac, Linux, phone or browser version.

On GitHub, download the installer using the download/raw button, not Save Page As.
Or click Code > Download ZIP, extract it, and open the play me folder.
The installer is unsigned, so Windows may identify it as an unfamiliar app.
The complete source is included in the repository if you want to inspect or build it.

QUICK CONTROLS
Xbox: left stick move, RT sprint, A snap. A/X/Y/B/RB pass to that receiver.
Tap an icon for a lob; hold and release for a bullet.
Move left stick slightly at release to place the pass.
With the ball: right stick left/right juke, down spin, up truck.
Y jumps after you cross the line. X dives. LB pitches backward.
Y before the snap opens the playbook. Menu pauses.
Click the right stick to throw away behind the line. View toggles mute.

Keyboard: WASD move, Shift sprint, Space snap, 1-5 pass.
Arrow keys juke/spin/truck, J jump, F dive, E spin, Q lateral.
Y before the snap opens the playbook. Escape pauses.
T throws away behind the line. M toggles mute.

HURDLES AND INJURIES
Time Y/J so you meet the defender in the middle of the jump.
Mistimed collisions can cause a fumble or injury. Injuries last 2-6 weeks.
An ambulance picks up injured players after the whistle. A/Enter skips it.
In-bounds plays use a 4-second runoff. Incompletions/out-of-bounds stop it.

YOUR TEAM
Choose EXHIBITION for a quick game. Choose FRANCHISE for your saved team.
Inside Franchise, open CLUBHOUSE. JOBS picks a club, including New Orleans.
MY COACH changes your name and pixel look. TROPHIES tracks your awards.
Use ROSTER to manage starters and reserves. New signings start on the bench.
The office also has trades, coaches, training, team colors, standings and playoffs.
12 teams. 11 regular season games. The top 6 make the playoffs; top 2 get byes.

NEW PLAY TOOLS
24 plays, including Read Option: LB/Q gives to the back, or keep and run.
Playbook > Create Play: pick a receiver, pick a route, press Use Play, then snap.
Save/Load keeps your play. Drag from a receiver to draw. Xbox: X opens drawing; hold A while moving the left stick, X undoes, B finishes.
Crowd ambience, touchdown cheers, big-hit reactions and whistles are included.
Pause > Settings has separate volumes, including whistles. Click/drag a bar
or use left/right. The field sound button also toggles mute.

SIM OPTIONS
Schedule > Sim Next Game
Pause > Sim Rest of Game
Playbook > Simulate Drive (before the snap)

Your team saves automatically. A live game can't be resumed after closing it.
Leaving an unfinished league game records a 0-21 forfeit.
Saves are separate from the installation, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Downfield.
Updating or reinstalling the game keeps those saves.

LATEST GAMEPLAY AND CLUBHOUSE UPDATE
RS click throws away behind the line. Keyboard T.
Before snap, RB / keyboard R offers the onside alternative: 4th-and-13
from your own 30. Convert to keep it. Fail and they get the ball.
After scores choose a normal kickoff or the same onside try.
The first attempt each game explains it. Any quarter, any score.

RBs can pitch back to the QB. If the ball never crossed the line and
no forward pass has been thrown, the QB can pass. Receivers resume routes.
Injured players use real reserves with their own jerseys. No backup
means a missing player. If all QBs are hurt, an existing player runs wildcat.

Clubhouse > Stadium + Money: set food and parking prices. Games and byes
have operating costs. Keep fans and the board happy or risk being fired.
Coaching Jobs lets a fired coach find work; your career record stays.
Stat Leaders has MVP/OPOTY races and passing, rushing, receiving tables.
Crowds use real CC0 recordings. Source credits are in the license.

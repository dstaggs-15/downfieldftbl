# Menu and Bowling references

Checked October 22, 2026.

The franchise navigation groups weekly play, roster decisions, staff and league information. That structure follows the general separation described in EA's [Franchise Hub guide](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/madden-nfl/franchise-hub/). Downfield uses its own pixel office, artwork and labels. Money, stadium prices and identity live under Team; contracts live on player cards.

EA's [playbook guide](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/madden-nfl/madden-playbooks-guide/) describes organizing plays around formations and team strengths. Downfield's preview now samples its own gameplay route functions instead of drawing generic arrows. Gold shows the intended carry lane; colored lines show receiver routes.

The original EA [NCAA Football 10 Xbox 360 manual, Mini-Games / Bowling, printed pages 10–11](https://www.videogamemanual.com/xbox360/NCAA%20Football%2010.pdf) confirms the opponent's 10-yard starting position, ten frames, two plays, first-play touchdown strikes and second-play touchdown spares. Open-frame scoring comes from yards gained, and field goals are excluded.

The [United States Bowling Congress scoring guide](https://bowl.com/keeping-score) supplies the standard strike, spare and tenth-frame bonus calculations. Downfield implements conventional ten-pin scoring, including twelve strikes for 300. Non-scoring attempts count whole positive yards; the next attempt starts with the remaining distance. Turnovers consume one attempt for zero pins. Teams alternate after each frame, with bonus plays staying in the tenth frame.

Downfield adds a CPU offense and shared-controller local play. This implementation uses Downfield's own football simulation and artwork. No EA game code or assets were imported.
